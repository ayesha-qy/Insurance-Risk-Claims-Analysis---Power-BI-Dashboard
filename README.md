# Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis** dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to help insurance companies analyze policyholder information, monitor claim patterns, and support data-driven business decisions.

The dashboard consolidates policy and claims data into a centralized reporting solution, enabling stakeholders to track key performance indicators, identify trends, and understand customer risk profiles through interactive visualizations.

---

## Business Objective

Insurance organizations often have policy and claims information distributed across multiple data sources, making it difficult to monitor performance and identify meaningful trends.

This dashboard addresses that challenge by providing:

- Centralized insurance reporting
- Interactive claim analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Risk assessment
- Executive KPI monitoring

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides the following business metrics:

- Total Policies
- Total Claim Amount
- Claim Frequency
- Average Claim Amount
- Gender-wise Policy Distribution

## Dashboard Visualizations

The report includes the following interactive visuals:

### Vehicle Analysis

- Total Claim Amount by Coverage Zone

### Customer Demographics
- Total Claim Amount by Age Group
- Total Claim Amount by Education
